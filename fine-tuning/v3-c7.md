C5's 139/200 included a malformed attempted call credited as a no-call. Two final repair targets concerned an extra restaurant rating argument and a malformed call on an irrelevant-tool prompt. C6 fixed both but lost nine protected tasks.

C7 starts from C5. Collect the last MLP's input activation at each selected prediction position. Solve for a weight change that corrects the repair targets, with covariance weighting from the preservation examples. Check the predicted token at the anchor positions. When a previously correct anchor changes, add a constraint that the weight update times that activation must be zero. Keep the accumulated constraints when solving again. The calibration set contained two repair sequences and 385 preservation sequences. Evaluate actual generations again after rounding to F16.

C7 preserved 279/279 required BFCL IDs and 40/40 required A6 IDs. The final analytical addition has rank at most three before rounding.
